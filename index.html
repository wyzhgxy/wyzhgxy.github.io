Yizhuo Wu

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), advised by Prof. Huan Zhang.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Peking University in 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Hao Dong as part of the PKU-Agibot Lab.

My research focuses on developing foundation models for generalizable, robust, and dexterous robotic manipulation in complex real-world environments, combining large-scale pre-training, computer vision, and reinforcement learning.
